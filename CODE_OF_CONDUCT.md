# Code of Conduct

Highfloor for Codex welcomes constructive participation from people with
different backgrounds, skill levels, and points of view.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining precise about impact.
- Give actionable feedback with enough context to respond.
- Respect privacy, attribution, license, and security boundaries.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and repository fit.
- Help keep technical disagreement proportional to the decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or demeaning language;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated off-topic demands;
- misrepresenting authorship, provenance, evidence, or security impact;
- pressuring contributors to bypass legal, security, or user-authority
  boundaries.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method
on the GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock
threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, impact,
and the safety of affected participants. Reports will be handled as privately
as practical.
