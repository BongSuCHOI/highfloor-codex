# RSS and Atom

Look for declared feed links in page metadata, conventional feed URLs or service documentation. A feed is useful for public article/post text, chronology and identifiers when the normal page is degraded.

Confirm that the entry matches the requested canonical URL, author and date. Feeds may contain summaries, stale cache or reordered updates, so do not claim full-page fidelity without evidence.

Do not use third-party feed proxies to bypass authentication or private-community access.
